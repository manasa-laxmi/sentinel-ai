SentinelAI is on

Send AI chatbots your questions, not your secrets

SentinelAI checks each message before it reaches an AI chatbot and lets you swap passwords, keys, ID numbers and personal details for safe placeholders in one click. Everything happens on your device.

What's new

  • Your real details come back in replies. The AI sees [PERSON_1]; you see the real name in its answer, on your screen only.
  • Names, health, salary and confidential text are now caught, in English and Hinglish.
  • Choose how careful it is: Relaxed, Balanced or Strict in Settings. Unsure items are shown as "possible" and never stop your message.

How it works

You keep using ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude exactly as before. SentinelAI only speaks up when something needs your attention.

Your privacy

A privacy tool shouldn't need your trust in yet another server, so SentinelAI doesn't have one.

Nothing leaves your deviceChecking, replacing and restoring all happen in your browser. There is no server, account or analytics.
Only counts are keptSettings shows how many items were caught by type. The actual values are never saved.
Placeholders are forgottenPairs like [PERSON_1] = your friend's name stay in memory only, and are wiped when you close the browser or click "Forget now".
One permissionSentinelAI only asks to save its own settings, and runs only on the five sites above.
Tip: pin SentinelAI to your toolbar (puzzle-piece icon, then the pin) to switch it on or off and see what it caught.
